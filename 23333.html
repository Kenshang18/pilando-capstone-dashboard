ANALYSIS AND DESIGN OF CABLE STAYED BRIDGE IN PILANDO, BUGUIAS, BENGUET, WITH SLOPE STABILITY ANALYSIS AND DESIGN

A rigorous structural and geotechnical engineering capstone study evaluating challenging mountainous terrain in Pilando, Buguias, Benguet. Integrating finite element cable-stayed bridge analysis, Bishop and Janbu limit equilibrium slope stability safety factors, soil nailing geotechnical stabilization, and Hybrid Multi-Criteria Decision-Making (MCDM/BWM) for optimal corridor and seismic damper selection.

Study Area & Geological Profile

Location: Pilando, Buguias, Benguet Province, Cordillera Administrative Region (CAR), Philippines.

Geology: Highly weathered fractured meta-volcanics and pyroclastic sequences overlying steep mountainous terrain susceptible to translational and rotational slope failures during seismic events and intense monsoonal precipitation.

Topography: Deep river gorge requiring a long-span superstructure (cable-stayed arrangement) to minimize mid-span pier construction on unstable valley slopes.

Structural Module

Cable-Stayed Bridge Structural Analysis

Real-time computation of stay cable tension forces and pylon bending moments.

Stay Cable Tension Force (Tcable)
Tcable = √Pdead2 + Plive2 + ΔTthermal
Pdead: Axial force due to dead load superstructure (kN)
Plive: Axial force due to live traffic load (kN)
ΔTthermal: Thermal gradient force adjustment (kN)
Pylon Maximum Bending Moment (Mu)
Mu = 1.25MDL + 1.75(MLL + MIM) + 1.0MEQ
MDL, MLL: Moments from dead and live loads (kN·m)
MIM: Dynamic load allowance (impact) moment
MEQ: Earthquake seismic moment (AASHTO LRFD)

Design Module

Cable-Stayed Bridge Component Design

AASHTO LRFD ultimate limit state proportioning for stay cables, composite steel girders, and reinforced concrete pylon.

Stay Cable Cross-Sectional Area (As)
As = Tultimate / (φ · fpu)
Tultimate: Factored ultimate cable tension (kN)
fpu: Specified tensile strength of tendon (MPa)
φ: Resistance factor for tension (0.90)
Pylon Reinforcement Ratio (ρg)
ρg = Ast / Ag ≥ 0.01 (Max 0.04)
Ast: Total area of longitudinal steel reinforcement
Ag: Gross cross-sectional area of pylon column

Geotechnical Analysis

Slope Stability Analysis (Bishop & Janbu Methods)

Factor of Safety (FoS) computation for Pilando hillside abutments under static and seismic conditions.

Bishop’s Simplified Factor of Safety (Fs) Equation
Fs = ∑ [ c'b + (W - ub) tan φ' ] / [ cos α ( 1 + (tan α tan φ') / Fs ) ] / ∑ W sin α
c', φ': Effective shear strength parameters
W, b: Slice weight and slice width kh: Horizontal seismic coefficient

Mitigation Module

Mitigation & Structural/Geotechnical Modeling

Implementation of linear viscous dampers, soil nailing grids, gabion retaining walls, and subsurface drainage systems.

Viscous Damper Damping Force Equation
FD = C · sgn(u˙) · |u˙|α
C: Damping coefficient (kN·s/m) | u˙: Piston relative velocity (m/s)
α: Velocity exponent (typically 0.3 to 1.0 for seismic energy dissipation)

Seesaw Linear Viscous Dampers

Energy dissipation during seismic events in Buguias fault zone.

Viscous dampers are installed at the deck-pylon interfaces. They provide velocity-dependent damping force, reducing seismic displacement response spectrum by up to 45% under maximum considered earthquake (MCE).

Geotechnical Soil Nailing & Shotcrete

Slope stabilization around abutment approaches.

A dense grid of 9-meter steel soil nails with reinforced shotcrete facing prevents surficial sliding and erosion along the steep roadway approaches in Pilando. Subsurface weep holes are integrated to relieve pore water pressure.
